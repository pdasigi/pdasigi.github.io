<p>I am a Lead Research Scientist on the <a href="https://allenai.org/olmo">Olmo</a> team
at the <a href="https://allenai.org/">Allen Institute for AI</a>. I have been actively involved in developing open language models, e.g.
<a href="https://allenai.org/olmo">OLMo</a> and <a href="https://allenai.org/tulu">Tulu</a> with a focus on post-training.
My current research interests lie in training (vision) language models for open-ended long-horizon agentic tasks including coding, search, and web browsing.
      
My <a href="research.html">prior work</a> spanned evaluation and training data curation for language models, improving the robustness and generalization of 
learning algorithms, and building question answering systems.

<p>I used to be a frequent host on the <a href="https://soundcloud.com/nlp-highlights">NLP Highlights</a> podcast along with other members
of the AllenNLP team.</p>
